PixelLoupe 1.4

## What and why

1.3 was about editing. 1.4 adds two big Develop and Photo Merge tools from Lightroom Classic and beyond: a **Remove** tool that can fill with an on-device AI model, and **Focus Stack**. It also adds **Catalog Settings**, with catalog backups, preview cache limits and duplicate detection. Vignette and grain now render like Lightroom's.

### Remove (Q)

- **A new Remove tool**, like Lightroom's. Press **Q** or click the eraser. **Spot Removal** stays as it was and moves to **⇧Q**. Both tools edit the same spots.
- **Three modes:**
  - **Remove** fills what you paint with new pixels.
  - **Heal** and **Clone** copy from beside the spot. Click for a round spot, or paint one. ⌘-drag on the photo picks your own source.
- **Use generative AI** fills with an on-device inpainting model (LaMa) instead of patch fill. Nothing leaves your Mac. The model isn't bundled: it's downloaded (196 MB) the first time you turn this on, with progress in the panel. Until it's ready, spots use patch fill.
- **Detect objects:** paint roughly over or around something, and the object found shows in red. Paint more with **Add**, take some away with **Subtract**, or hold **Option** to paint the other way. Click **Remove** or press Return; Esc cancels. With Detect off, what you paint is removed as soon as you let go.
- **Once a spot is made** you can switch its fill between Remove, Heal and Clone, change its Opacity, or click **Refresh** for a different fill.
- **On the photo:** the selected spot is outlined so the result shows through. Pins can be dragged. **Tool Overlay** (Always, Selected, Never) controls when pins show. A pin pulses while its fill is being made.
- **Zoomed in,** hold **Space** to pan, in both Remove and Masking.

### Develop's tool row

- **Presets and Lens Corrections are now sections of the Adjust panel**, as in Lightroom: Profile, Presets, the other sections, then Lens Corrections.
- The row is now **Adjust, Lighting, Crop, Masking, Remove, Spot Removal, History**, with larger icons and a dot under the active tool.

### Focus Stack

**Photo ▸ Photo Merge ▸ Focus Stack… (⌃F)** merges photos of one scene focused at different distances into one that's sharp throughout. It's also in the grid's right-click menu.

- **The Focus Stack sheet** shows the preview as **Original**, **Rendered** or **Depth Map**. Click it to see that area at 100%.
- **Source Photos** lists the photos in the order they were taken. Uncheck one to leave it out. Photos that don't line up are marked and left out.
- **Three rendering methods:**
  - **Depth Map** (the default) takes each pixel from the frame that's sharpest there. Best for most stacks. **Radius** and **Smoothing** tune it.
  - **Weighted Average** blends every frame by how sharp it is, so there are no seams. It can leave a blurred patch beside a near edge over a far background.
  - **Pyramid** keeps overlapping detail at different depths, such as hair, branches or mesh. It can add a little glare around bright edges.
- **Focus Stack with Previous Settings (⌃⇧F)** stacks straight away with your last settings.
- Lining up adjusts for the lens's change of magnification as focus moves. Stacks of RAW photos are saved as `<name>-Focus.dng`, others as a 16-bit `<name>-Focus.tif`, beside the earliest photo and stacked with its sources if **Create Stack** is on.
- **It's fast:** a 90-photo, 60 MP stack is lined up and previewed in about 8 seconds and merged at full size in under 30.

### Catalog Settings (⌥⌘,)

**PixelLoupe ▸ Catalog Settings…** opens a sheet for the open catalog, as in Lightroom. The settings are saved inside the catalog.

- **General:** where the catalog is (**Show** reveals it), when it was created and how big it is.
- **Previews:**
  - **Standard Preview Size** (Auto, which follows your largest display, or 1024 to 2880 px) and **Preview Quality** (High, Medium, Low). Existing previews are redrawn at the new size as you view them.
  - **Limit Preview Cache Size:** when Standard previews go over the limit, the least recently viewed are removed. Thumbnails and Smart Previews are never removed.
  - How much space previews and Smart Previews take.
- **Metadata:**
  - **Include Develop Settings in metadata inside JPEG, TIFF, PNG and PSD files** (off by default). Exports, Publish, Edit In and web galleries then carry the photo's settings in a form Lightroom and Camera Raw read.
  - **Automatically detect duplicates for all photos** (off by default). Photos whose original files are identical show up in a new **Duplicates** item in the sidebar's Catalog section. Checking reads every original in the background, with progress under the grid. Nothing is ever removed.
- **Backups:**
  - **Back Up Catalog:** Never, monthly, weekly (the default), daily, every time PixelLoupe exits, or just the next time. When a backup is due, PixelLoupe offers it as you quit, with an option to test the catalog's integrity first. **Skip This Time** asks again at the next quit.
  - Choose the **Backup Folder** (by default `Backups` beside the catalog) and how many to keep. Older ones are removed.
  - The pane lists your backups, with **Show in Finder** and **Delete…**.
  - A backup is a zip of the catalog without its previews. Unzip it and open it with **File ▸ Open Catalog…**.

### Closer to Lightroom

- **Post-Crop Vignette** now matches Lightroom Classic. Before, a −60 vignette left the corners far brighter than Lightroom's. All three styles, Highlights, Midpoint, Roundness and Feather are fitted. Vignettes you made before 1.4 keep their old look; new ones, presets and Lightroom imports use the new one.
- **Grain** now matches Lightroom's in strength, size and roughness, at any export size. As with the vignette, photos that already have grain keep their old look.

### Sliders

- **Click a slider's value once** to type a new one. Before, it took a double-click. Double-clicking the label or knob still resets it.
- **Crop's Angle** can now be typed too.

### Exports

- **PSD exports now carry metadata.** Before, they had none.
- **An original's own Camera Raw settings no longer leak into exports.** A JPEG edited in Camera Raw used to pass Adobe's settings on to its export.

## Before you upgrade

- **Catalogs opened in 1.4 can't be opened in 1.3.** The catalog moves to model v21, a one-way migration. Back up your catalog first if you might need to go back. (1.4's new Backups can do this for you from now on.)
