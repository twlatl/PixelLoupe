PixelLoupe 1.4.2

- **Camera profiles (`.dcp`) can now be imported and used.** In Develop, the Presets panel's options menu (…) has **Import Profiles & Presets…**, which takes DNG camera profiles as well as presets. They appear in the **Profile** menu after PixelLoupe's own looks, for every RAW photo, whatever camera they were made for. So film-look profiles people publish for one camera, such as the [Fujifilm-style looks for the Sony α7C II](https://github.com/twlatl/PixelLoupe#camera-profiles-develop), also work on an α7 IV or α7CR.
- **How they render:** from the profile's look table and tone curve, after Exposure and White Balance and before everything else. **Amount** scales the look. PixelLoupe renders through Apple's RAW engine, not Adobe's, so a look is recognisably itself but won't match Lightroom exactly.
- **Import Lightroom's Presets** also brings in Lightroom's own camera profiles (Camera Standard, Camera Vivid and the rest). Each is listed under **Camera Matching** for its own camera. Adobe looks built on one of them now render on it, and an imported Lightroom catalog uses it where a photo was set to it.
- **Fixed:** camera-matching profiles and presets made for one camera weren't offered on Sony and Fujifilm photos.
- Catalogs are unchanged from 1.4, so there's nothing to do before upgrading.
