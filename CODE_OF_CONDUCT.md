# Code of Conduct

PixelLoupe follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, and focus on what's best for the project and the people in it.
Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated anywhere the project lives (issues, pull requests, commits).

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@twlatl](https://github.com/twlatl), privately. Reports are kept
confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, or block people, who don't follow this code.
